Vaayil is a calm, private client for the WireGuard® tunnels you already have: a server you run yourself, or a configuration from a provider you trust. Vaayil sells no bandwidth, runs no servers and has no accounts.

BRING YOUR OWN TUNNEL
• Import a .conf file, paste a configuration, scan a QR code, or enter the details by hand. You can also open a .conf file in Vaayil from your file manager or another app.
• Connect with one tap. The dashboard tells you at a glance whether your tunnel is up and has completed its handshake, with honest session totals and the time of the last handshake.
• A Quick Settings tile and a home-screen widget turn Vaayil on and off, and the app icon's long-press menu connects any of up to four profiles.
• Works with Android's Always-on VPN and "Block connections without VPN".

SPLIT TUNNELING
• Choose the apps that bypass the tunnel, or the only apps that use it. Changes apply right away.
• Allow local network keeps printers, cast devices and other devices on your local network reachable while you're connected.

ENCRYPTED DNS
• DNS over HTTPS and DNS over TLS through an in-tunnel resolver.
• Split DNS: answer chosen domains with your current network's resolver instead.
• Vaayil warns you when Android's Private DNS is set to a hostname, because apps then send their DNS lookups there instead of to your profile's DNS.

AUTOMATION
• Connect automatically on Wi-Fi networks you haven't marked as trusted, on mobile data, or when the phone starts.
• Auto-connect on untrusted Wi-Fi needs location access set to "Allow all the time", because Android shows the current Wi-Fi network's name only to apps with location access, and the check runs in the background. Vaayil reads the network's name and security type (on Android 11 and older, it finds the security type in the list of nearby Wi-Fi networks), keeps no history of networks, and never sends network names or location anywhere. With this option off, Vaayil never asks for location.
• Optional: start and stop tunnels from automation apps with explicit intents, protected by a per-install token.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads.
• Private keys are encrypted at rest with a key held in the Android Keystore, when the device's Keystore is available.
• Android cloud backup is off for Vaayil. Move to a new phone with an encrypted, passphrase-protected backup file that you export yourself.

COMPLETELY FREE
• All features, with no trial limit or in-app purchases.

ABOUT THE VPN PERMISSION
Vaayil uses Android's VpnService to run the WireGuard tunnel you configure. Connections through the tunnel are encrypted from your device to your tunnel's endpoint. Vaayil does not collect, log, sell or share what you send through the tunnel, your DNS queries or your browsing.
