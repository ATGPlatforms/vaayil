New in 1.1: a Quick Settings tile and a home-screen widget turn Vaayil on and off, and the app icon's long-press menu connects a profile. Allow local network keeps printers and cast devices reachable while you're connected. Open a .conf file in Vaayil from another app. Vaayil warns when Android's Private DNS is set to a hostname, which sends apps' DNS lookups there instead of to your profile's DNS. Also: more precise wording about connections, and dialogs that stay usable at large text sizes.
