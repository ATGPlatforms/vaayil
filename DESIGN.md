## 1. Overview

Vaayil's interface is vellum: a warm paper board on which sheets of paper are
laid, one film at a time. Where security tools shout — neon greens, terminal
blackout, militaristic locks — Vellum builds a warm, domestic calm: a cream
canvas deepened by soft paper films, sage and slate and lavender ink accents,
cut-paper seams instead of shadows, and clear honest telemetry. The app should
feel like a well-made household object that happens to guard your traffic.
Security is conveyed through peace, not urgency: the hero disc, the state
pills, and the numbers answer "am I protected?" in one glance, then get out
of the way.

## 2. Colors: The Vellum Palette

Every surface is the bare board — `canvas #FBFAF7` — with paper "films" laid
over it. One film darkens the surface by a fixed perceptual step; the ladder
ships as six static hexes (above), never runtime alpha math. Depth in code is
a film count, not a color choice.

### The film ladder
`canvas` (0 films) → `container-1` (cards, row groups) → `container-2` (nested
blocks, badges) → `container-3` (wells, hero seat) → `container-4` (segment
track, switch off-track) → `container-5` (the floor). Content nesting stops at
three films; a fourth is a crash, not a color. **The Film Rule:** one film =
ΔL −0.0206 ±0.0008, ΔC +0.0050 ±0.0012, hue 85° ±6° in OKLCH, held by unit
test (`VellumTokensTest`). Pressing adds one film.

### Ink
Primary ink `on-surface #23221D` (15.3:1 on canvas); secondary ink
`on-surface-variant #57544A` (≥5.3:1 at every nesting depth). Disabled content
is `#85827A` (3.68:1 — exempt from 1.4.3, meets 3:1 anyway).

### Boundaries
Load-bearing boundaries are always `stroke-3 #7C7264` (≥3.3:1 against every
ladder step): clickable sheets, switch rings, error rings, focus rings
(`primary #3E5A42`, 2dp). `hairline #DDD4C2` (1.41:1) is for inset dividers
and `seam #CEC2A9` (1.51:1) for the cut edge — both **decorative only**; a
boundary that must identify a control carries stroke-3 or a text label.

### Accents
**Sage means sanctuary:** `primary #3E5A42` marks active/verified states and
affirmation actions (7.3:1 on canvas); `primary-container #CFE0CE` with
`on-primary-container #22371F` for selected pills; `sage-mark #4A7350` for
strokes and marks (sparkline, active dot, connected ring). `slate #3F5C78`
governs transport data. `lavender #5A4E78` accents DNS/advanced. Avatar
pastels `#DCE8D9` / `#D8E3EE` / `#E2DCEC` cycle by profile id, glyph in the
paired on-color (≥8.9:1).

### Semantic tones
Connected `field-active #DCE8D9` / `on-field-active #28402C`; disconnected
`field-idle #EAE4D8` / `on-surface-variant`, dot `idle-dot #7C7264` (3.73:1 —
never a mid grey). Warning: surface `#F3E2C3`, text `on-warning #5E4218`
(7.3:1), stroke `warning-mark #B9762B` — **stroke only, never text** (3.5:1).
Error: surface `#F7DBD4`, text `on-error #7B241C` (7.6:1), stroke
`error-mark #A93A31` (6.0:1); inline field errors use the M3 error slot
`#BA1A1A`. Dialogs and dropdowns sit on `scrim` = `#23221D` at 44%.

## 3. Typography

Schibsted Grotesk everywhere — a neo-grotesque with narrow advances and tight
fit. Bundled weights: 400, 500, 600, 700 only (the 11sp uppercase `label` is
the sole 700; everything else runs 600 or lighter). Its four properties drive
the scale: it reads optically light below ~14sp (hence the 700 label), fits
~5% more characters per line (hence the larger display/headline), needs extra
tracking in uppercase (+0.115em), and needs no negative display tracking
beyond a hair.

- **Hierarchy:** display 30/600, headline 21/600, title 16/600, body 15/400,
  bodySmall 13/400 (secondary copy only — cap it at two lines), annotation
  12/500, action (button labels) 15/600.
- **Numerics & network telemetry** use `telemetry-num` (21/600) with OpenType
  tabular figures (`tnum`) so live speeds and byte counters never jitter.
- **Labels:** uppercase tracking is reserved for small technical micro-copy
  (`label` style) — protocol tags (`WIREGUARD`), section eyebrows, badges.
- **The Data Speaks Mono Rule** (amended 2026-09-21, device review C4): endpoints, keys,
  IPs, DNS servers, stats, and rule targets render in platform monospace, everywhere —
  the shared mono text style (`monoTextStyle()`) pins 14sp/20sp with zero tracking (at
  15sp the glyphs ran 25% wider than the Schibsted cap height; at 14sp the digits sit
  just under it). Smaller mono usages are sanctioned per-component exceptions: package
  names 13sp (Split Tunnel rows) and the version string 12sp (Settings footer).
  Machine values wrap at their break opportunities — after `.` and `/` and before `:`, the
  shared `MachineValueText` — so a port stays with its colon wherever the space allows; a
  segment wider than the space it gets still wraps mid-token as a last resort — never worse
  than an unbroken Text; semantics keep the plain value (2026-10-02 sweep 2, U2).

## 4. Depth: The Film Rule

Depth is layered paper, not light and shadow.

- **Resting sheet:** its ladder color, a 1dp `seam` cut edge, no shadow.
  The seam is ONE CLOSED perimeter (amended 2026-09-21, device review): a
  single `drawRoundRect` stroke — 1dp, `seam`, inset 0.5dp, corner radius
  r−0.5dp — the same geometry as the `stroke-3` control outline, drawn via
  `drawBehind`. The retired top-and-left-only stroke read as an open bracket
  on real panels, so every edge closes now; abutting sheets still never
  double-draw to 2dp. Sub-48dp decorative tiles (top-bar logo square,
  profile avatar) carry fill only — no stroke.
  `Modifier.border` is banned: it strokes all four sides and all four
  corners, double-draws where sheets abut, and never lines up with seams.
- **Lifted sheet:** the one shadow in the system — `sheet-lift`:
  elevation 6dp, ambient `#3A301C`, spot `#3A301C` @ 60% (Android-native;
  the platform's ~0.039/~0.19 alpha scaling yields ~3.9%/~11% effective —
  amended 2026-09-21, device review C6: the web token's 6%/8% alphas
  rendered at ~0.2%/1.5%, invisible) — resets to bare canvas. Reserved for
  dialogs, dropdowns, and focused fields (the bottom bar is docked since
  2026-09-21 and casts nothing; the Split Tunnel search pill stopped
  lifting in C6 — it is a filled field). Nothing else casts a shadow.
- **Nesting:** cumulative film depth is capped at three; the composition
  local enforces it and a violation fails fast.
- Onboarding sheets stack with a 4dp overlap offset so each film's seam stays
  visible.

## 5. Components

### Tab header
Every tab opens with the one shared `TabHeader` (device review 2026-09-21, C9/X1):
title in `headlineSmall` (18/600) 12dp below the top bar; when an intro exists,
`bodyMedium` `on-surface-variant` 4dp under the title; content starts 16dp below the
intro — the trailing gap is baked into the header, callers never pad after it. The
optional trailing action slot aligns to the title's FIRST BASELINE, so a tall control
rises above the title line instead of dragging it off the grid.

The title never breaks inside a word: it is measured against the whole line and wraps at
its spaces, and the action moves under it when the two cannot share a line (2026-10-02
sweep 2, U3).

### Hero connect disc
112dp disc in a 14dp-radius three-film seat, full-bleed to the 20dp gutter.
Disconnected: the disc is bare canvas — *lighter* than its surround, a window
to the board — with a 1dp seam ring and a 36dp `on-surface-variant` glyph.
Connected: the window fills `primary` with a `#FBFAF7` glyph and a static 1dp
`sage-mark` ring at 132dp. Sage means verified: a live tunnel with no handshake
10 s after connecting keeps the canvas window, and the status pill reads
"Waiting for handshake" in WARNING. Pressed seat adds one film; a failed start
shows a 2dp `error-mark` ring. The label under the glyph stays on one line and
caps its font scale at 1.15, the largest that clears the window's round edge
(F29).

### Status pills, badges, dots
32dp pills (radius 999) with a 7dp breathing dot: ACTIVE
`field-active`/`on-field-active`/`sage-mark`; IDLE
`field-idle`/`on-surface-variant`/`idle-dot`; WARNING `warning`/`on-warning`;
ERROR `error`/`on-error` — never white-on-clay. The breath is opacity-only
(2400ms, 1.0→0.6) and goes static under reduced motion. 32dp is the floor:
at large font scales the label wraps and the pill grows rather than clip it
(F29). Micro-badges are `container-2` chips at 6dp radius with a seam and
11/700 uppercase labels.

### Telemetry ledger
The dashboard's paper-ledger rows (`DOWN` / `UP` / `HANDSHAKE`) wear
`labelSmall` uppercase labels (the 11/700 `label` style) — not `body` — with
`telemetry-num` values; an unset fact renders "—" in `disabled`. Uppercase
micro-copy renders through `VellumEyebrow` (`:core:ui`), which uppercases its
input — the 11/700 style never sets mixed-case text (device review
2026-09-21, C4); sentence-case labels wear `titleSmall`, and the mono face
reads 14px/20px so digits sit just under the Schibsted cap height. Eyebrows
stay on one line; only a warning card's title wraps at large font scales.
When a ledger value no longer fits beside its label, it drops below it. The
facts ledger (`ENDPOINT` / `ADDRESS` / `DNS`) gives each label its natural
width and the value the rest of the row, right-aligned and wrapping; a value
that cannot fit beside its label drops under it with its lines start-aligned
(device check 2026-10-03): no fixed value column, nothing truncated (F29).

### Sheets and buttons
`Sheet` lays a film (default one, 10dp radius, seam on, 20dp padding);
clickable sheets carry 1dp `stroke-3` — they are controls. Primary button:
`primary` fill, `#FBFAF7` action label, 52dp, 10dp radius; pressed adds 8%
ink, never scale. At large font scales a label that cannot fit wraps and the
button grows (F29). Secondary: `container-2`, seam, `on-surface` label. Quiet
actions are text + 1dp seam underline on a 48dp target; pressed, the
underline darkens to stroke-3.

### Form fields
Wells are `container-3` with the closed 1dp seam perimeter, 8dp radius,
label above. Focus
lifts the well out of the stack to bare canvas with `sheet-lift` and a 2dp
`focus-ring` — the most legible focus state in the app. Error rings are 2dp
`error-mark`, supporting text `m3-error`. Machine values render in mono.

### Lists, switches, segments
App rows sit directly on the bare canvas at 68dp with hairline dividers inset
past the icon — the Split Tunnel tab is one lazy list whose header scrolls away
with the content, so no row clips at a card edge (device review 2026-09-21,
C8). An app's name stays on one line and ends in an ellipsis; at large font
scales the selection count keeps its line and Route All / Bypass All wrap
below it (F29). Switches: on = `primary` track + canvas thumb; off =
`container-4` track
under a `stroke-3` thumb, the track ring in `stroke-3` too (3.54:1 — the
familiar M3 off-knob; the canvas off thumb vanished at 1.28:1, and M3 offers
no thumb-border parameter, so the ring lives on the track alone; device
review 2026-09-21, C9). The segmented control is a
`container-4` pill at 52dp whose selected segment is a *lifted* canvas pill —
physically raised, not recolored — with radio semantics.
A saved profile row keeps its controls beside the text only while the text's
longest unbreakable piece — a whole word of the name or a whole machine-value
segment — fits beside them, or when its endpoint would need more than two lines
beside them; otherwise the controls drop to their own end-aligned line under the
text (U3's rule; device check 2026-10-03, 332dp at font scale 2.0; ruling 1,
2026-10-03). The active profile's card is that profile's row while it is active,
so it keeps the row's connect switch at the end of its `field-active` header
strip: on while the tunnel is connecting or connected, and a tap turns it off.
It follows a saved row's large-text rule: beside the header text while that
fits, otherwise on its own end-aligned line under it (J6, 2026-10-16).

### Navigation
Settings opens with Protection, followed by Automation and About. Vaayil is free;
Settings has no License section, trial status or payment controls. The existing films,
typography and spacing apply unchanged.

The bottom bar is docked — the floor of every tab: full-width `container-1`
fill with a 1dp `hairline` rule along its top edge, no radius, no shadow,
clearing the system navigation bar via `navigationBarsPadding()`. 68dp of
content (8 + 32 + 4 + 16 + 8) carries four equal destinations whose whole cell
is the touch target — no ripple; the capsule is the state feedback. 68dp is a
floor: the bar grows with its label line at large font scales. The
selected destination wears a 56×32dp `primary-container` capsule (radius 16)
behind the 24dp icon only; labels sit below the capsule: 12sp/16sp, +0.010em
tracking, sentence case — weight 600 `on-primary-container` selected, 500
`on-surface-variant` unselected (`VellumNavLabelStyle`), each label and the
capsule keeping ≥8dp side margins inside the cell at normal text sizes. Labels
stay on one line and cap their font scale at 1.15, so all four fit side by
side in a 360dp window with ≥4dp margins; an ellipsis guards only narrower
windows (F29). The top bar sits on the
bare board: 24dp logo in a `container-2` square (fill only — sub-48dp tiles
carry no seam), wordmark at titleLarge, quiet gear.

### Dialogs and overlays
Dialogs, dropdowns and the QR share are lifted sheets over the scrim; the QR
ink is pinned `#3E5A42` (7.65:1 on a pure-white mat with a 20dp quiet zone).
Warning and error cards use their semantic surfaces with a
`warning-mark`/`error-mark` seam; warning body text is `on-warning`, never
the mark.

### Motion
Press and film transitions are **instant tone swaps** in the shipped code; the 90ms / 200ms figures recorded in
`DESIGN.json` describe design intent, not shipped timing. Two things animate, both as state feedback. The hero
disc's fill crossfades between canvas and sage over 260ms when the disc gains or loses its sage
(`animateColorAsState`, Compose's default FastOutSlowIn easing), and snaps under reduced motion. The status dot
breathes (2400ms opacity 1.0→0.6, repeat-reverse) and goes static at 1.0 under reduced motion; its opacity steps
about ten times a second, not every frame, which keeps an idle screen cheap (G2). Nothing slides, nothing scales.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- Do keep every surface light: canvas plus films — the palette has no dark
  mode, ever.
- Do build depth with films and seams; pressing adds one film, nothing
  scales.
- Do reserve sage for sanctuary (active/verified states + affirmation
  actions); lavender accents DNS/advanced; slate governs transport data.
- Do render machine data in monospace and telemetry numbers with `tnum`.
- Do use `stroke-3` for any boundary that must identify a control; hairline
  and seam are decoration.
- Do honor reduced motion (`LocalReducedMotion`): fills swap instantly, dots
  sit static; feedback is preserved, movement is not.
- Do show real telemetry only; empty slots stay quiet ("—") rather than
  decorative.

### Don't:
- Don't re-introduce dynamic color, dark themes, or wallpaper-derived
  palettes.
- Don't use `Modifier.border` — seams are the shared closed-perimeter
  stroke, drawn via `drawBehind`.
- Don't cast a shadow anywhere except a lifted sheet; sheet-lift is the
  system's only elevation.
- Don't stack films past three — the fourth film is a development error, not
  a color.
- Don't put warning-mark or error-mark on text; marks stroke, on-colors
  carry text.
- Don't use neon, terminal aesthetics, fear-urgency copy, fake speedometers,
  or globe maps; don't decorate with error red or warning amber.
- Don't add motion beyond state feedback; nothing slides, nothing scales.
- Don't reach for a dialog when an inline explanation will do.
